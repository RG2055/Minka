# Dither art

Card pictures `skin-art-*.webp` and header bands
`../header-backgrounds/pool/art-*.webp`, made by `scripts/build-dither-art.py`
(levels + unsharp mask, then Atkinson / Floyd-Steinberg / Bayer / line-screen
dither in one ink; Floyd-Steinberg and tone controls after lenxism/dither, MIT,
https://github.com/lenxism/dither, licence in
`kalendars/assets/licenses/LICENSE-lenxism-dither.txt`).
