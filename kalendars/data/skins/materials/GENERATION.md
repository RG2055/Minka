# Depth backgrounds

Created with the built-in imagegen tool. Original transparent images were resized to 720 × 720 and encoded as WebP; alpha is preserved. No runtime segmentation or animation is used.

## alpine

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a premium wallpaper foreground asset on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, square 1024x1024. No text, no numbers, no people, no UI, no frame. A dramatic single pale granite Alpine mountain with finely detailed rock and subtle snow on the summit, lit by soft peach dawn light. Mountain peak near x=34% y=37%, rocky ridgeline descends toward right edge to y=70%. Mountain and foothills completely fill the bottom third to both bottom corners. Top third entirely transparent. Strong naturally irregular silhouette, photographic detail, restrained silver pink and blue shadow tones. It will be overlaid in front of giant clock-like digits on a card, so upper right and upper middle must remain mostly transparent.

## peony

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create an elegant wallpaper foreground asset on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, square 1024x1024. No text, digits, people, UI, frame or vase. One large lush pale blush peony blossom, macro botanical photography, exquisite softly illuminated petal detail with a few dark sage leaves. Blossom centered x=30% y=64%, width=66% of canvas. One curving stem trails downward off bottom edge. Top 28% and rightmost 20% mostly transparent. Asymmetrical photographic composition, dramatic quiet studio side lighting, creamy blush and burgundy petal shadows. Designed to partially occlude oversized digits behind its top-right petal silhouette.

## cat

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a beautiful wallpaper foreground asset on a genuinely transparent background with alpha, square 1024x1024. No text, digits, humans, UI or frame. A sleek charcoal black domestic cat seated in a relaxed three-quarter side profile, ears upright, looking toward upper right. Photographic fine fur texture, amber eyes, subtle warm copper rim light making silhouette visible. Cat head centered x=34% y=46%, body occupies lower left/center, bottom cropped at canvas bottom, tail curves along bottom. Entire subject bounded mostly x=12% to 65%, ears start y=28%. Top quarter and rightmost third remain transparent. Sophisticated editorial animal portrait, quiet dark espresso warm palette. Designed as foreground covering part of a giant number behind the cat.
