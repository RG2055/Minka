# Statue dither art

Card pictures `skin-statue-*.webp` and header bands
`../header-backgrounds/pool/statue-*.webp`, made by
`scripts/build-statue-dither.py`: the sculpture was cut out of its photo with
the Mac's Vision (`scripts/cutout.swift`) and dithered to one ink on a plain
ground with a port of lenxism/dither (MIT, https://github.com/lenxism/dither,
licence in `kalendars/assets/licenses/LICENSE-lenxism-dither.txt`).
