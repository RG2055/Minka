# RG Radio skin sources

The runtime assets in this folder are locally cropped and compressed to 2400 × 400 pixels.
The 720 × 120 `*-preview.jpg` derivatives keep the selector lightweight; the full image is loaded only for the selected skin.

- `neon-city-2400x400.jpg` — “A futuristic city at night with neon lights” by Nat, [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/dA0-qxdbyyY), Unsplash License.
- `deep-ocean-2400x400.jpg` — “Artistic depiction of dark ocean waves” by Kris Møklebust, [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/34155536/), Pexels License.
- `mist-forest-2400x400.jpg` — “Moody Forest Landscape with Misty Atmosphere” by Berke Çetin, [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/37265747/), Pexels License.
- `golden-horizon-2400x400.jpg` — “Ocean waves under orange sunset” by Raimond Klavins, [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/ghgI8CjEYCo), Unsplash License.
- `red-storm-2400x400.jpg` — “Dramatic red clouds fill the dark sky” by Rafael Garcin, [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/nYfXQzvtMP4), Unsplash License.
