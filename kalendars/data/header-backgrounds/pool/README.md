The collection ("Kolekcija") the header draws from. Each picture is tagged in
MIX_POOL (kalendars/js/calendar.js) with the parts of the day it suits and with
its focal point — the object-position that keeps its subject in view whatever
the header box crops.

Sizing: the header box is the same height on every screen, so it is about
5.7:1 on a laptop and 9.4:1 on a 1900px-wide monitor. A picture cut to 12:1 or
wider is therefore shown almost whole on a wide monitor and is only cropped at
the sides on a laptop, which is what these are for. The older pictures are
2400x433 (5.5:1) and are stretched around their focal point on very wide boxes;
the 2026-09-22 ones are 2400x250 (9.6:1) and need neither.
